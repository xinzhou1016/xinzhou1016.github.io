Xin (Stella) Zhou

I am a PhD student in Marketing at Bocconi University and an International Research Fellow (Visiting PhD Student) at China Europe International Business School (CEIBS), Shanghai, from September 2026 to February 2027.

My research focuses on digital marketing and AI, examining how digital technologies reshape markets and consumer behavior. I study platform design, creator incentives, network effects, and the value of AI-enabled services.

My advisor is Kai Zhu, and my co-advisor is Sara Valentini.
